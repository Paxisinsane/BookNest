The Dusk Buster Adventures

By Sam Milne

Sam set out on his faithful ship, the Dusk Buster towards what he thought to be the tomb of Attila the Hun. Until this time, no one had been this close to finding the secret, triple coffin, burial place of the ancient Mongolian’s tomb. And after some years he saw ahead through the fog an island with a cave. He looked anxiously at the tattered map he had been led by for years, could this be it? After what seemed like an eternity He brought his long boat ashore. He wrapped an oil soaked cloth around the end of a wooden club and lit it at the mouth of the cave. , He sees foot prints leading in after brushing off some cobwebs he went in two. And he saw carvings of war tribes fighting with cities and walked in a little more, In to the cave and saw more carvings but instead of seeing more fighting he saw Burial drawings and after entering a corridor he saw oil lamps lit as if someone was expecting him. He put down his torch, then turned to find the most confusing thing, he saw foot prints leading straight into a wall. It seemed as if the fog had cleared because the cave seemed a little brighter than before And he noticed a little pond and in it was a coffin that seemed to be made of. . .gold? He became very excited and ran to get a rope [and the fog had cleared] and after a lot of doing [he had the help of his crew] it was out, when it was open he found yet another coffin and he knew that this was the tomb of Attila, the Hun. And when he’d opened the third coffin he saw Attila the Hun. After another lot of doing, they got the coffin out of the cave When they got home, Sam was still wondering why the outside coffin was made of gold, He thought that the outer coffin was made of iron, then silver, then gold. So, in two weeks he once again set out on his trusty long boat the dusk buster. I think it’s a-a,well a Chinese Dragon, I think it is, what were we talking about? You were saying we can’t tell anybody about this. And so they sailed home, and when Sam got home he had a hot bath and went straight to bed. When he got up in the morning thinking about the foot prints and why they led to a wall.
